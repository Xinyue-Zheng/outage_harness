Built on 2026-11-15 from the arXiv listing. Nothing is ranked or judged, and I have read none of these papers for this list.

## How the list was built

- Queries on arXiv: `harness` in the title; `agent harness`, `agentic harness`, `harness engineering`, `model harness` or `harness design` in the abstract; `context engineering` in the title.
- Kept: papers submitted from 2025-10-01 on, whose title or abstract has the word "harness" (not "harnessing") or whose title has "context engineering", and whose title or abstract mentions agents, LLMs or language models.
- Not removed: papers that use "harness" in another sense (test harness, fuzzing harness, evaluation harness, robot policy harness). You will see them in the list.
- Not included: blog posts, engineering posts and open-source projects without a paper; papers outside arXiv.
- Part 1 has the 578 papers with "harness" in the title, with the start of the abstract. Part 2 has the 505 papers that mention it only in the abstract, as titles.
